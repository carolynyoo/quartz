### Steps
1. Gather flowers, foraging and harvesting with reverence
2. Get a crystal or glass bowl
3. Fill with spring water (or filtered, distilled). If buying, get from glass bottle
4. Float flowers on top of water in bowl
5. Soak in moonlight (full moon recommended, especially with lunar herbs)
	1. 3-4 hours minimum
6. Strain flowers, preserving water
7. Create mother essence with 1/2 infused water, 1/2 brandy
8. Create a stock essence through 2/3 brandy, 1/3 water, and 7-13 drops of mother essence
9. Dilute the mother essence a max of 3 times 

Subtle blend!

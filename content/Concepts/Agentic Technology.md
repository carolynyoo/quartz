Tech that encourages human #agency: freedom to act within the digital environment, with ownership and expression. 

Contrary to agentic AI, which focuses on the agency of _artificial intelligence_ (e.g. a chatbot that can book flights for you). 

##### Non-agentic tech: 
Social media platforms (rented space, metaphor of marketplace)

##### Agentic tech:
Allows [[Folksonomy]]
Decentralize / decenter 

Currently preventing more agentic software: 
Security measures requiring complex backend and auth

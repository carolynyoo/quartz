**The inner masculine side of a woman**, one of [[Carl Jung]]'s twin concepts of human psyche (the other is [[Anima]])

## Ways of becoming aware of animus
* Dreams
* Active imagination
* Archetypal imagery 
* Projections onto others

## How to feed one's animus
* Questioning borrowed opinions from familial or social pressure
* Sitting with own authority, taking up space
* Pursuing goals for their own sake, not for service or worth
* Cross-examining one's inner critic (where is self-criticism or doubt coming from? what is the evidence?)
* Act from logos or yang (order, forward movement)

Japanese literature genre - a confessional, stories depicting events from author's life 
More personal and emotional than autobiographies 
Blurs line between narrator and author

### History
Intellectual exchange starting again through Meiji restoration after period of isolation under Tokugawa shogunate. Shoyu Tsubochi, a scholar and proponent of Western ideas, heralded fiction as a valuable genre providing insight into characterization and interiority, which he thought lacking in Edo literature. 
In the late 1800s after Meiji Constitution emphasized divine power of the emperor, scholars turned towards individualism and I-Novels were a form of resistance through which the author could express individuality. 
Now in the west we have [[autofiction]]. Classically could also be similar to #proust

### Framework
* Written from the I
	* way to express hidden beliefs, desires, experiences 
* Reality (jijitsu)
	* Author's experience = story in novel
	* Reflection of author's spiritual condition (inner reality more important than accuracy of facts #introspection)
	* Appears natural and unplanned to readers
* #Sincerity (makoto)

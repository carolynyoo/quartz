From [Barbara L Frederickson](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1693418/)

Positive emotions (joy, interest, contement, love) broaden an individual's thought-action repertoire into novel and creative acts that build resources and improve survival:  
* Joy -> play
* Interest -> exploration
* Contentment -> savoring, integration
* Love -> recurring cycle

Negative emotions tend to narrow action (attack, flee)

Coined by French writer Georges Perec in 1973 essay "Approaches to What". I discovered this while reading Jenny Odell's [[Saving Time]].

Instead of the *extraordinary* that news and media focus on, the *infraordinary* is in the invisible and habitual. 

> "That which is generally not taken note of, that which is not noticed, that which has no importance: what happens when nothing happens other than the weather, people, cars, and clouds."

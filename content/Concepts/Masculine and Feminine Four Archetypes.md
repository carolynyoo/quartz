## Static Feminine
* Elemental image from uterus: dark, moist, holding to gestation
* Great Mother
* interdependent 
* undifferentiated
* Mother Complex
* *Positive aspect*: evolution that makes life possible
* *Negative aspect*: indifference to individuality, resistance to change

## Dynamic Masculine
* Elemental image from phallus
* Initiatory, action-oriented toward goal
* Mating, hunting strategy
* Hero, master, conqueror 
* *Positive aspect*: cognitive development, linear expression, humanistic technology
* *Negative aspect*: harm, destruction, violence, disregard for nature

## Static Masculine
* tendency toward organization and systems
* Herd mentality
* Patriarchal culture, hierarchical social order
* Preservation* of order, adaptation over authenticity
* Father Complex
* *Negative aspect*: order and organization for its own sake, dehumanization, bureaucracy 

## Dynamic Feminine
* Undirected movement
* Playful flow of experience, responsiveness
* Reciprocity of nature 
* Creative process
* Maiden anima beckoning towards new unions, insights
* *Positive aspect*: transformed awareness, participation and process
* *Negative aspect*: altered state of consciousness that leads toward disintegration and chaos. drug dependence, hysteria, identity diffusion, psychosis

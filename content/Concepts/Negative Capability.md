Pursuit of questions, of ideals that can't be answered, even when it leads to uncertainty. Capability of *living* in uncertainty. Rejection of set philosophies and preconceived systems of nature

Concept by English poet John Keats


Relates to Rainer Maria Rilke's quote:
>Be patient toward all that is unsolved in your heart and try to love the questions themselves, like locked rooms and like books that are now written in a very foreign tongue. Do not now seek the answers, which cannot be given you because you would not be able to live them. And the point is, to live everything. Live the questions now. Perhaps you will then gradually, without noticing it, live along some distant day into the answer.

From Mary Oliver's *A Poetry Handbook*:
> Only by remaining himself negative, or in some way empty, is the poet able to fill himself with an understanding of, or sympathy for, or empathy with, the subject of his poem

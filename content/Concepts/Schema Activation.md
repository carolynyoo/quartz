In order to learn we need to integrate and activate certain context to understand it and encode into long-term memory.

Complex learning needs multiple schema and context switching

Schema is also cultural and emotional. Having preconception of something being boring, difficult, or "not for you" affects engagement and thus learning. 

### Problems of wrong schema
* If the wrong schema is activated it hinders the learning process and affects memory retrieval
* Beginners can sometimes learn better than having incorrect schema, since schema is sticky and needs reorganization

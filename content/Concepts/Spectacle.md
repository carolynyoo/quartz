A feature of late capitalism where life is perpetually viewed secondhand, mediated through a third party (e.g. "main character energy" concept from Tiktok is an encapsulation of this)

* Communication -> phone
* Travel or connection -> vicarious Youtube watching, parasocial interactions

Ties to [[workism]] where appearing like you're working and important is valued just as much as actually doing it. [[Workism is a spectacle.]]

You can't escape the spectacle. Even if you escape knowledge work, the spectacle will swallow you into another pressure or form. 

> “Complacent acceptance of the status quo may also coexist with purely spectacular rebelliousness—**dissatisfaction itself becomes a commodity** as soon as the economy of abundance develops the capacity to process that particular raw material.”

The spectacle can be undermined or hijacked—e.g. by choosing slow, analog, human paths. 

Term from Guy Debord's "*[The Society of the Spectacle](https://files.libcom.org/files/The%20Society%20of%20the%20Spectacle%20Annotated%20Edition.pdf)*", discovered through "[Why is Everyone in Tech So Sad?](https://www.noemamag.com/why-is-everyone-in-tech-so-sad/?utm_source=substack&utm_medium=email)", _Noema Mag_

A quality of greatness or awe, which requires some risk. Often attributed to philosopher Immanuel Kant in his _Observations on the Feeling of the Beautiful and Sublime_. 

### Qualities of the Sublime
* Noble
* Splendid
* Terrifying
* Lives outside of human-made object 

The sublime is inherently difficult to grasp in magnitude. The sublime is not made with a particular purpose in mind (contrast this with "beauty"). 

With the sublime, a human is forced to come to terms with the limits of human achievement. It's a "confrontation between inward fear and outward mastery" (from [[Hanging Out]])

"The feeling of the sublime is a feeling of displeasure that arises from the imagination's inadequacy, in an aesthetic estimation of magnitude, for an estimation by reason, but is at the same time also a pleasure."

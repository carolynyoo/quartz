From philosopher Josef Pieper's *Leisure: The Basis of Culture*, discovered through Jenny Odell's [[Saving Time]]

Definition: fundamental interruptions that run at "right angles to work" which is seen as horizontal time (e.g. the five day work week + weekend)

One needs to step out of horizontal time to experience true leisure, not R&R for work's sake

Vertical time is awe-inspiring, gratitude inducing, an example of kairos

# Types of edges
* Crisp edges, found closer up
* Soft edges, found further away 
* Jagged edges, commonly found in nature
* Disappearing edges (e.g. Rose Hilton, Bonnard)

## Edges in art eras
* Impressionism - soft, broken color
* Surrealism e.g. Magritte - crisp, graphic

## How to soften edges
* Blot with paper towel
* Try wetting aid, like Helen Frankenthaler (needs to be horizontal)
* **Glazing**: transparent layer with glazing medium
* **Scumbling**: scrub area with busted brush, dry opaque layer

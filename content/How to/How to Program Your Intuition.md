Train [[Reticular Activating System]] and subconscious through repetition. 

# Steps
1. Make sure you are calm and centered. Do not do this when overwhelmed or on the go. 
2. Write or speak a clear, emotionally meaningful intention. Be clear about time constraints if you have them. 
3. Repeat it daily—morning and before sleep. 
4. Visualize the desired state. What are you doing, seeing, and *feeling*? 
5. Ask insightful questions. 
	1. What's the next step I need to take? 
	2. What would this look like if it felt easy? 
	3. Who would I be if this were easy? 
6. Review patterns and synchronicities as they come up. Intuition often speaks through repeated messages. Keep a log of these. 


### Examples
* I want clarity about my next step.
* Help me notice what I need to move forward.
* Help me with this project in the next 3 days.
* Show me all aligned opportunities.

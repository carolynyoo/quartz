### Preview the book
Do 5-10 minutes of pre-reading. Read the book jacket, read some historical context. Limit this so it doesn't completely sway your views, but do a bit of study so you're not setting false expectations from a book. 

### Predict the book
What is the book going to be about? What's the tone (funny, dark, sad)? 

### Identify purpose of the book
Is it to educate, entertain, persuade? 

This is so we set the right [[Schema Activation]] for optimal reading experience.

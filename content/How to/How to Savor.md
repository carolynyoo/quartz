Lean into positive emotions, focusing on the good in the present moment. 

### Ways to Savor
1. **Bask**: Being open to praise, celebrating one's self
2. **Gratitude**: Looking for things to be grateful for. List making. Expressing thanks
3. **Awe**: Marvel at things, feel wonder, lose self in moment
4. **Luxuriate**: Use all five senses to truly enjoy the present moment

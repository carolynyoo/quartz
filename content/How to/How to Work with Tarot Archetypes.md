Jungian or archetypal tarot deals with #individuation, reflecting and exploring self to strengthen it and transcend ego, rather than divination, predicting the future and relying on it as external wisdom. 

See tarot practice as **conscious exploration of the unconscious**. 

## Active Imagination Technique
1. Clear environment and call up the tarot card you want to channel. Observe the image and note anything of interest. 
2. Allow the archetype to guide you through a waking dream.
3. Record what you see and feel. 
4. Analyze any insights or changed relationship to the card. Any revelations?

MBTI, Enneagram, Human Design, astrology, numerology. #cyclicalframework 

Here's why we love frameworks: 
1. We love to detect patterns in people. We compare and contrast, form judgments, and use metaphors and archetypes to understand. It's a way to hack the brain so it doesn't have to think so hard to understand something completely foreign every time. 
2. Dopamine hit from recognition, from being seen. I mean, isn't this why people are increasingly talking to AI? (which is literally built for pattern matching)
3. There's an anxiety to not having a clear definition of the #self. We seek to conceptualize a selfhood 

What makes a good framework: 
1. Clear definition
2. Internally consistent structure
3. Broad scope

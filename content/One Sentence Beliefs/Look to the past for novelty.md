So much of desire to me is associated with novelty. I want new things—new books to read, movies to watch, journals to fill up. A lot of this is programmed desire, decades of training and influence from consumerism. 

To satiate my desire while actually providing something *new*, not just a repackaged new that is barely different from what I have just consumed, I can look to the past for inspiration. 

## Where to look for the past as new
* Past journals
* Past sketches, drawings
* Public Domain Archive
* Book I've already read

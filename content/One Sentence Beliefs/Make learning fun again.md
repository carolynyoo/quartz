If you're in school to produce, then you might think using AI to generate your homework might seem okay. But if you're in school to learn, then you realize the output is not the goal. 

Most students don't realize that. Output was the goal since that's what resulted in the grade! Grading must have changed since AI. 

People worry about [American students getting dumber](https://nymag.com/intelligencer/article/american-adult-lower-iq-scores-cognitive-decline-technology-flynn-effect.html). To fix that, we need to make learning fun again.

Ways in which society are making learning fun again:
* Studyblr
* Personal curriculum

It's not just about consumerism. There is a real moral tidal wave to save our human intelligence.

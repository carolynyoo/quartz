ELEMENT: FIRE 

Community, togetherness, relaxation, openness, connectivity, excitement, nurturing, performance, sharing, enthusiasm, fulfillment, validation, interdependence, celebration, collaboration, ease

- Share time, space, work
- Commune with people, see art, meet makers
- Grounded time of surplus that doens’t ask us to rush

### Practices

- Creative community, connection, and collaboration
    - Prompts
        - where do you feel you most belong?
        - what acts of service do you regularly perform?
        - who do you want to thank?
        - name some teachers and what they teach you
        - what is the difference between a friend and acquaintance?
        - is there a group to join that can enrich creative practice?
    - List ten people you want to connect with this summer. What are your creative ties? How could they uplift your voice? Is there a new craft that could harmonize with your current one?
- Creative conversation
    - dinner party
        - hear about others’ creative concepts, receive energy
        - ask everyone to bring a short poem to share
        - salon style: share WIPs
        - themed dinners to inspire creativity
        - bring item to group altar
        - conversation topics - draw from a hat
- Know your audience
    - Who is ideal audience? Seek editor or workshop group
    - Event to showcase finished work
    - The action can be small
    - What can you learn about your artwork thru the way others witness and receive it?
- Plan your performance
    - Envision and write five scenarios of inventive, inspirational performances
    - DIY tours
- Stealing time for practice. Remember to have fun!
- Align with the sun (cold baths, watermelon)
- Shedding
    - Adjust discipline to adapt with weather
    - What can be put down to better fill arms with pleasures and delights of season
- Summer cleanse (juices, fruits)
- Swimming
    - **“Let creativity be like water.”**
    - Cleanse, cold baths
- Fullness (drink freely, relish)

### Month by Month

June: find good pause in project to turn toward social plans. tie up loose ends to lean into spontaneity 

Summer Solstice: Root into outward state of being. Solstice party! Intention of extroversion

July: Time to travel, indulge in community, do a short residency 

August: final trips, luxuriate in the heat, spoil yourself with swims and friends. Tune into creative focus a bit as summer ends 

Summer → autumn: be slow as you turn inward, do not rush. Steal moments of summer that still exist

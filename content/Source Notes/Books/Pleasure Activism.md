Covering topics from sex and drugs to race and climate change, _Pleasure Activism_ emphasizes the importance of feeling good as the basis for creating any kind of lasting change. I’ll speak from my own experience—centering pleasure in my current season of life is helping me act more in my integrity, shed guilt and unworthiness around rest, and generally be a happier, healthier human being. Don’t we all want that for each other?

First #autotheory book? Combines autobiography, memoir, critical theory. It's a way to bring in the personal while grounding and making accessible the heaviness of trauma with the context of theory

The book is rooted in two main events: Harry's T procedure and Maggie's pregnancy. 

### Takeaways 
* Articulation itself is its own form of protection
* The inexpressible is contained in the expressed
* Repeating is inevitable. Dependency is inevitable.

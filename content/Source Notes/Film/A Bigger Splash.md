## Summary
Expected a straightforward documentary and was surprised that it was a Warhol-esque "improvisatory narrative-nonfiction hybrid" film. Reads as early reality television but the drama is non-verbal. Instead it's artsy gay sex scenes and Hockney tearing through his canvas in frustration. Hockney apparently hated this movie and tried to pay director Jack Hazan to not release it! 

The movie features his breakup with his muse Peter Schlesinger (of Portrait of an Artist painting fame—he's the guy standing over the pool) so it's quite intimate, even if not completely real.

## 3 D’s

Most to dos we procrastinate on are actually _projects_.

Project = any task with more than one action step

Calling mom vs. planning mom’s bday

[Magic ToDo - GoblinTools](https://goblin.tools/)

3 D’s:

- Delegate
- Defer (to person or tool)
- Delete

🪄DO: “Do I need to do this or am I making things more challenging than they need to be?” 80/20!

## Low-information diet

If you’re tap dancing on precipice of burnout, recommend implementing a low-information diet for 30+ days.

- No educational content—pause any classes related to job or work
- No work-y podcasts—gossip and silly goose content only!
- No work-y social media
- No documentaries
- No nonfiction
- Limit news intake

Replace with:

- Hobbies
- “Junk food” media (comedy podcasts, movies, kpop)
- Fiction
- Socializing IRL
- Exercise
- Making bad art
- Making bad anything
